## What are levels?

Levels are subspaces that collectively form the Backrooms. Levels can be diverse in shape, size, and form; their properties and appearance are their most differentiating features that makes them unique from one another. For example, certain levels may be entirely outdoors, whereas other levels may be completely indoors, and some levels can be open worlds.

While places within [the Frontrooms](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/The_Frontrooms "The Frontrooms") have a defined climate, location, and time, all three of these things cease to apply for the levels of the Backrooms. Levels may fluctuate over time, slowly changing their properties, and may stop existing entirely, as demonstrated with [temporary manifestation](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Phenomenon_99 "Phenomenon 99") and [Level 379](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_379 "Level 379"). Levels are home to the various properties documented across the Backrooms, most notably [entities](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_List "Entity List"), [objects](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_List "Object List"), [phenomena](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Phenomenon_List "Phenomenon List"), and other assorted features.

Levels may also have sub-levels, which are variations of a level. They are similar and hold a direct connection to their parent level, but lack enough distinguishing qualities to be designated as their own level.

**Numbered levels** are levels that were the first to be documented with the [Casper-Bray Numbering system](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Phenomenon_13 "Phenomenon 13"). Their respective [level keys](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Object_2 "Object 2") emit a resonant frequency that can be picked up by a radio when tuned up to a certain frequency; the number of that frequency is the number that the level is designated under. Numbered levels also possess anomalies that are based on their number, and one may feel that they are going "higher up" the Backrooms as they progress to numbered levels with higher numbers. Aside from that, numbered levels are easily identifiable by their typical liminal feeling and are known for their relative stability.
