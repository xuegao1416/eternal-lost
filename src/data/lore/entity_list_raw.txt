## What is an entity?

**Entities** are supernatural creatures inhabiting the Backrooms. They are classified into three categories: their number, name, and habitat. "Number", as it may suggest, refers to the numerical notation of an entity; "name" is what the entity is known as; lastly, "habitat" refers to how often you will encounter the entity.

For convenience, [M.E.G.](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/M.E.G. "M.E.G.") has assigned every non-level based entity a number. In this list, entities are organized by numbers. Some are classified as a sub-entity or variant of an individual entity species because they resemble one another in appearance and/or traits.

## Entity Habitats

*   **Unknown**: The entity's presence is hidden in an unknown state.
*   **Level (#)**: The entity is found only in its main habitat/level. These are classified as Level-based.
*   **Majority but mainly on level (#)**: The entity is found in a majority of levels but mainly its main habitat/level. These are classified as Level-based.
*   **Extinct**: The entity has been wiped out.
*   **Nearly Extinct**: Very few of the entity remain and are mostly found in hidden levels.
*   **Rare**: The entity is found in less than 5 levels.
*   **Uncommon**: The entity is found in at least a dozen levels.
*   **Common**: The entity is found on more than 20 levels.
*   **Majority**: The entity is found on over 50% of levels.
*   **Many**: The entity is found in a myriad of levels.
*   **The Backrooms**: The entity is found on all levels.
*   **Obfuscated**: The entity's location is deliberately concealed, making it a well-guarded secret within the levels.
*   **N/A**: The entity's location can't exactly be explained and/or doesn't fall under the previous categories however the entity does exist.

 "CE" means "Catalogued Entities." This designation is used for certain parts of the list where vast sections have only a few cataloged entities. As a result, the list in those parts has been shortened to just the catalogued entities. You can still add new ones.
